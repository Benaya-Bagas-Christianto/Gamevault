# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Indonesian gamers searching for, purchasing, and managing games. They appreciate a factual, highly structured, and technical environment where utility, precision, and performance are prioritized over marketing fluff.

## Product Purpose
TokoGame is a digital game storefront and management platform (inspired by Epic Games' layout) optimized for Indonesian gamers, delivering high-speed catalog browsing, game details, cart/wishlist management, and library checkout.

## Brand Personality
Precise, Factual, Engineering-grade, Structured, Technical. Highly structured layout with crisp gridlines, clear typography, and a "Technical Documentarian" tone.

## Anti-references
SaaS-modern / Bubbly UI (rounded pill/oval buttons, soft heavy drop shadows, decorative color gradients, atmospheric mesh gradients, bubbly floating card animations, large border-radii, and unnecessary visual ornamentation).

## Design Principles
1. **Engineering-Grade Structure:** Layouts are aligned to a strict, visible grid, prioritizing density, alignment, and data clarity.
2. **Angular Precision:** Zero-tolerance for rounded pill/oval shapes. Interactive elements have a maximum radius of 2px or are completely sharp (0px).
3. **Flat Paper-White Canvas:** High-contrast, flat, physical paper-white background with crisp black ink boundaries. No soft shadows, no decorative gradients, no atmospheric background glows.
4. **Factual & Focussed Utility:** Component proportions are rigid and predictable (e.g. 1:1 square icon buttons). Interaction is immediate, explicit, and lacks bloated animations. Hover states use clean underlines and sharp high-contrast fills.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **High Contrast & Keyboard Navigability:** Strict adherence to WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios (using dark ink on paper-white background).
- **Clear Focus Indicators:** High-contrast, visible, angular focus rings on keyboard navigation.
- **Reduced Motion Friendly:** Immediate transitions or minimal high-contrast indicator shifts, with absolute zero bubbly or floating motion.
